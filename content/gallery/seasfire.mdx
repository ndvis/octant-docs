The SeasFire Cube gathers climate, vegetation, ocean and human variables with burned
area and fire emissions on one space-time grid, built to study and forecast wildfires
weeks to months ahead.

Open it in Octant to browse its variables, animate along time, or draw any of them on
the globe.
